## Remaining limitations

Passing tests, review and a startup readback do not prove long-term forecast
accuracy, seasonal robustness, calibrated P10/P90 or battery-control suitability.
Replay uses archived weather to isolate learning, and cannot reconstruct original
forecast issue times. Weather still depends on fresh provider input; historical
reuse must never turn stale input into a new network receipt.
